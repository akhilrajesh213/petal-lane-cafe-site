Privacy Policy

Petal Lane Café · Last updated 11 October 2026

Petal Lane Café is a cozy merge game for Android. This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have. If you have a question, email patellanecafe@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone

Your café (board, coins, gems, stars, level, story progress and settings) is saved on your phone. Reminders are scheduled on your phone by Android and are only shown if you allow notifications. None of this needs an account.

Cloud save

To protect your café if you lose or change your phone, the game backs it up to our cloud service. When the game starts for the first time it creates an anonymous account for you, with no name, email or password. We store:

  • An account ID: a random identifier for your anonymous account.
  • Your game save: the same game progress listed above, and when it was saved.
  • An install ID: a random identifier made by the game on your phone, so we can tell which phone a save came from if you play on more than one.
  • Your email address, only if you choose to add it (Settings > Cloud save > Save to my email). We use it only to send you one-time codes that link your café to that email and let you restore it on another phone. We don't send newsletters or marketing.

We use this information only to back up and restore your café. We don't sell it or use it for advertising.

Play data and crash reports

To find out which parts of the game players enjoy, where they get stuck, and what goes wrong, the game sends anonymous play data and crash reports to Google's Firebase:

  • Gameplay events, such as starting and finishing the tutorial, reaching a level, finishing a room task or chapter, claiming a daily reward, running out of energy, watching a rewarded ad, and what you buy in the in-game shop with coins or gems.
  • App and device information such as app version, device model, Android version, language, approximate country (from the IP address), and how long and how often you play.
  • Crash reports: what the game was doing when an error or crash happened, with the device model.
  • A Firebase app instance ID: a random identifier for this installation, so sessions can be counted. It isn't linked to your name, your email or your cloud save account.

We don't collect your advertising ID for this, and this data isn't used for advertising. You can turn it off at any time with Settings > Share play data; the game then stops sending play data and crash reports.

Ads

The game offers optional rewarded ads: you can choose to watch an ad, for example for extra energy. Nothing plays unless you ask for it. The ads are provided by Google AdMob, which uses your device's advertising ID, your IP address, device information and how you interact with the ads to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and some US states), the game asks for your consent before any ads are shown, and you can choose non-personalised ads; you can change that choice at any time with Settings > Privacy choices. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads).

Purchases

You can buy gems and a starter pack with real money. Payment is handled by Google Play: we never see your card or payment details. To deliver what you bought and restore it on a new phone, the game records each purchase (what was bought, when, the store's order number and price) with our purchase service, linked to your cloud save account ID.

Who processes your information

We use service providers to run the game:

  • Cloud hosting stores the cloud save and the accounts.
  • Email delivery sends the email with your one-time code, if you add an email.
  • Bot protection checks that a real person, not a bot, is creating a cloud save account or asking for a restore code, using technical signals from your device such as your IP address and browser details.
  • Purchase management records purchases so they can be delivered and restored.
  • Google: Firebase receives the play data and crash reports, AdMob shows the optional ads, and Google Play processes payments. Google uses this information under its own policies (how Google uses information from apps that use its services, Google privacy policy).

These providers may process data on servers outside your country. Except for Google's use of ad data described above, they act on our behalf and may not use your information for their own purposes.

How long we keep it

Play data is kept by Firebase for up to 14 months, and crash reports for 90 days. Purchase records are kept for as long as the law requires us to keep sales records, also after you delete your cloud data. Your cloud save and account are kept while you use the game. You can delete them at any time (see below). Deleting the game from your phone removes the save on your phone but not the cloud backup; to remove the backup, use the in-game option before uninstalling, or email us.

Deleting your data

In the game: Settings > Cloud save > Delete my cloud data. This immediately deletes your account, your email address (if added) and your cloud save. Full instructions, including how to ask by email, are on the account deletion page.

Security

All data sent between the game and our services is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Each account can only read and change its own save.

Children

Petal Lane Café is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us their email address, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your information, or to object to how it's used. Email patellanecafe@gmail.com and we'll reply within 30 days.

Changes

If we change this policy we'll update this page and the date at the top.
