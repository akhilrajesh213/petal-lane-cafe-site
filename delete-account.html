Delete your Petal Lane Café account

Petal Lane Café · Last updated 11 October 2026

You can delete your Petal Lane Café account and cloud save at any time, either in the game or by email.

In the game (quickest)

  1. Open Petal Lane Café.
  2. Tap the Settings button (the gear, bottom left).
  3. Tap Cloud save, then Delete my cloud data.
  4. Tap Delete to confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away. The café on your phone stays playable, and cloud save stays off until you turn it on again.

By email (if the game is no longer installed)

Email patellanecafe@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Petal Lane Café account", sent from the email address you added in the game (Settings > Cloud save > Save to my email). We'll delete the account linked to that address within 30 days and reply to confirm.

If you never added an email address, your account is anonymous and we can't tell which one is yours. In that case only the in-game option above can delete it. Accounts that never had an email address hold no personal details, only game progress.

What is deleted

  • Your account and its account ID
  • Your email address, if you added one
  • Your cloud save (game progress) and the install ID stored with it

What is kept

If you bought anything with real money, the record of each purchase (what was bought, when, the order number and price) is kept by RevenueCat and Google Play for as long as the law requires sales records to be kept. Nothing else from your account is kept after deletion. The email provider that sent your sign-in codes (Brevo) may keep a short delivery log of those emails for a limited time, under its own privacy policy.
